What the manual is

Level Up: Make Your First Game with AI walks a student from a first idea to a small game they can actually play. Each level is a quest with a goal, a hands-on activity, an AI prompt you type yourself, a boss challenge, and a parent note.

The first tool is Microsoft MakeCode Arcade. It is free, it runs in the browser, and a child does not need an account to start. The rule of the book: AI is your sidekick, not your controller. You type. Your student decides what to keep.

Level 1 is free to read and print for your own family. Read Level 1, then come back here for the paperback or the PDF.
