Level Up Makers: Pocket Games

Kids make retro handheld-style games in GB Studio. A parent stays with them.

Coming soon. Price coming soon. This book is not for sale yet. It is a separate manual from the three-step learning path.

Download free Level 1 Get notified

Level 1 of Pocket Games is free. It is the first lesson, not the full book. The rest of the book is coming soon, and the price is not set yet.

What the student builds

Pocket Games is a Level Up Makers manual for kids who want to make games that look like the ones on a retro handheld. Across ten levels, they build those games in GB Studio.

GB Studio is free. It runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux. A parent runs the lessons.

Ages

Kids, with a parent nearby. The lessons stay on the family computer. This page does not ask a child for a name, a photo, or an account.

What you need

A Windows, Mac, or Linux computer. GB Studio is a free desktop app for those systems. These levels are not a browser lesson.

  • GB Studio. Free, from the GB Studio site. You install it.
  • A parent. You stay with your child for the lessons.
  • AI coding help, optional. A parent decides whether to use it. If you do, it stays on the parent's account. The student does not make an AI account for this book. The rule is the same as the other Level Up Makers manuals: AI is your sidekick, not your controller.
  • A real retro handheld, optional. One payoff, after the levels, is playing the finished game on a real retro handheld cartridge-style device. That device is the family's choice. The book can be finished on the computer. This page does not sell a device.
